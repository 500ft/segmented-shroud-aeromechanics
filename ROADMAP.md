# Roadmap

## Closed by the owner

The owner ended this project on 6 October 2026 and authorized merging the
remaining documentation and closing the repository. This closure supersedes
all earlier work orders, pending owner-input requests and future-stage plans.
There is no active next step.

The research finish line was a seam/step comparison at matched total thrust,
with a reference-checked baseline and stated uncertainty. That comparison was
not completed. Closure is a decision to stop; no aerodynamic null, practical
equivalence or validated design is claimed.

## Preservation

The repository retains original data, scripts, frozen acceptance records,
uncertainty/status revisions and historical plans. Unfinished ledger entries
remain as recorded; their historical status does not authorize further work.
The [WIP snapshot](https://github.com/500ft/segmented-shroud-aeromechanics/tree/c4f6b5268298fd18c2c0d3ae618a18338e3c1c93)
and original local worktrees are preserved.

The proposed bench-switch choices H1-H4 were never adopted. They are no longer
outstanding action requests for this closed project. No rotor, equipment budget,
minimum useful effect or approximate reconstruction was approved through closure.
Any future restart requires a new explicit owner decision and review of the
retained evidence. The [decision log](docs/decision-log.md) records the closure.
