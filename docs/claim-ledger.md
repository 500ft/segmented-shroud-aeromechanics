## Evidence-state vocabulary

- **Literature:** supported by a cited external source.
- **Planned:** specified but not built or run.
- **CAD:** geometric model only.
- **FEA/CFD:** computational evidence under stated assumptions.
- **Measured:** physical evidence under a documented protocol.

Concept art and proposed geometry are never hardware evidence.
