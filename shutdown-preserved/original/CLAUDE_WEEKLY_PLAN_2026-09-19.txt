CRITICAL DESIGN IMPROVEMENTS OVER THE CURRENT A0 PLAN

1. Treat the validation ladder as a claim ladder. A0.1 validates a 2D airfoil workflow. A0.2 validates open-rotor loading for a particular rotor. Neither validates duct tip-gap flow. Until A0.3 exists, every Study A result must carry the label rotor-validated, gap-unvalidated.

2. Verify source numbers before building geometry. The current case file intentionally contains nominal values that must be checked. Claude must not promote nominal values into verified values without page, table, figure, or data-file locators.

3. Separate formal validation uncertainty from sensitivity spread. GCI measures numerical discretization uncertainty. Clocking variation is a deterministic sensitivity unless a probability model is declared. Turbulence-model spread is model-form sensitivity and is not automatically an independent random uncertainty. Do not mechanically call the RSS of these terms a confidence interval. Before Study A, create an uncertainty decision record that says which terms are formal uncertainty, which are sensitivity bounds, and how the decision band is constructed.

4. Use a two-band reporting rule. Report a formal validation comparison band U_val for comparison with measured data, using the documented ASME V&V 20-style components available from the source. Separately report a conservative Study A decision band U_dec that includes numerical, clocking, and model-form sensitivity. If independence cannot be defended, use a stated conservative envelope or retain the terms as separate bands. A null means not resolvable by this model and mesh, not proof that seams have no physical effect.

5. Do not accept an apparently converged solution without monitor evidence. Each run needs residual histories, thrust and torque histories, stopping criterion, final averaging window, and a statement about whether the monitored quantities plateaued. A low residual alone is not convergence of the engineering quantity.

6. Treat data digitization as an uncertainty source. If published curves must be digitized, retain the original PDF hash, page/figure locator, digitization method, point file, and digitization uncertainty. Do not compare extra decimal places than the source supports.

7. Keep the prior-art lane separate from the CFD lane. A close-reading record must not be inserted into the historical day-3 file. A patent route search must not be inferred from opening one known public patent page. Screening, close reading, patent searching, CFD validation, and physical qualification are different evidence classes.

8. Make the duct gap limitation visible at every decision point. The validation ladder is intentionally useful but incomplete. If A0.2 passes, it permits a labelled rotor workflow; it does not permit a paper sentence that says the solver is validated for segmented ducts.

  How: Specify the rotor axis, origin, angular zero and direction, units, reference profile, measurement section or axial sampling rule, and the rotor envelope used for static clearance. Register the averaging convention before generating variants. Define c(theta) only where the relevant shroud wall exists; retain a separate domain mask for missing-wall seams. Record seam endpoints and angular sampling/refinement rules so a narrow seam cannot disappear between sample points.
  Required checks: Equal mean under the declared convention; minimum static clearance over the declared domain; two-lobe amplitude and phase; seam count, width and azimuth; radial-step magnitude and position; bounding box; expected solid/component count; valid topology; and volume. Check reference datums and support blockage across comparison configurations. Do not invent a finite clearance inside a missing-wall seam or use an arbitrary mask normalization to claim equal mean.
  Done: Every metric has a definition, unit, expected-value method, predeclared numerical tolerance and failure behavior. Separate numerical geometry tolerance from fabrication tolerance and measurement uncertainty. Static design clearance is labeled as such; CAD cannot establish running clearance or measured performance.

CAD-P3 — Specify independent checks and deliberate failure cases.
  How: Hand-check simple nominal cases, then compare generated geometry with an independently derived expectation. Reading the same reviewed parameters is acceptable; computing expected metrics from the candidate's measured output, or reusing the same geometry-construction helper on both sides, is not independent verification. CadQuery may independently check SOLIDWORKS, but a CadQuery generator still needs an independent analytic or separately derived check.
  Positive cases: Continuous reference; zero-amplitude two-lobe equivalence; zero-width seam and zero-height step limits under the registered definitions; representative valid defect cases.
  Negative cases: Rotor intersection; missing or invalid units/dimensions; missing fit-critical inputs; a seam narrower than the initial angular sample spacing; a misplaced defect with unchanged volume; stale exported geometry after a parameter change; and corrupt or wrong-unit STEP input.
  Done: The plan pairs each case with the specific check expected to pass or fail. Volume agreement alone cannot accept shroud geometry. The very small numerical differences reported in the briefing are observations from its examples, not transferable tolerances or physical accuracy claims.

CAD-P4 — Require evidence that parameters drive the saved and exported part.
  How: For each exposed dimension and positional driver, choose a valid perturbation with a predicted geometric consequence. Regenerate, save, reopen and measure; then export STEP and inspect it in a fresh process. Verify intended changes and the relevant invariants. Include seam azimuth and step position, since a volume-only check cannot detect their movement. Preserve the reviewed baseline and use separate output locations for perturbed cases.
  Done: A parameter-to-metric table identifies the perturbation, expected response, measured response and verdict for native and neutral geometry. A parameter is not demonstrated as editable merely because its displayed value changed.

CAD-P5 — Adapt host execution only if the native-authoring route is selected.
  How: First read engineering-audit/docs/host_setup.md; before SOLIDWORKS COM code, also read docs/solidworks_api_findings.md. Verify signatures and return semantics for the installed API version. Probe sketch-to-global orientation with known geometry and a bounding-box check. Inspect per-feature return values and equation acceptance; record incremental geometry metrics to localize failures. A successful rebuild or plausible preview is insufficient.
  How: Reuse the documented host runner, record job identity and expected artifacts, and allow long overall completion times with short polling intervals. Distinguish pending, failed and completed jobs. On timeout, check whether the original job remains active before launching a duplicate. Reopen saved files and validate geometry; file existence alone does not establish correctness.
  Done: The proposed host smoke test has a known expected result, actual completion evidence, installed-version record and explicit timeout/failure handling. Exact commands are taken from the pinned shared-tool revision and verified there; the racing-mast commands are examples, not ready-made shroud commands.

CAD-P6 — Preserve input provenance and acceptance state through release.
  How: Each parameter records its value, units, source locator, evidence route and unresolved status. Distinguish design_then_inspect, vendor_drawing and measurement. A nominal design choice stays provisional_design until its required evidence exists. A drawing does not satisfy a measurement requirement without a prospectively recorded route amendment. Missing critical inputs block the affected operation; nominal design-only inputs cannot silently become fabrication inputs.
  How: Plan one evidence manifest per accepted case: input revision/hash; generator and shared-tool revision; software versions; native and STEP identities; commands; measured and expected metrics; tolerances; re-drive results; verdicts; and limitations. Hashes establish identity, not geometric correctness. If an independent expected-value check is missing, preserve accepted: null with a reason; a performed check that fails is a failure, not an unknown or pass.
  Done: SSY-CAD-07 can distinguish a nominal design review, a geometrically verified artifact, and a fabrication release using traceable evidence. Keep CAD_TASKS.csv as the sole CAD status ledger.
